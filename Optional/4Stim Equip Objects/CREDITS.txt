4Stim Equip Objects (optional add-on, docs/ACTORS.md)

Strap-on: OStim NG's "Calyps Strap-On" (meshes and textures by Calyps,
3BA conversion by VersuchDrei), from OStim NG (GPL-3.0,
github.com/VersuchDrei/OStimNG). Refit to Fallout 4's True Wasteland Body
for 4Stim by tools/refit/build_strapon.cpp: the harness takes TWB's weights
and sliders, the dildo is weighted to ZeX's Penis_00-05 bones so it bends
with 4Stim's penis bend.

Build it in BodySlide ("4Stim OStim Strap-On (TWB)") with your preset, and
enable "4Stim - Equip Objects.esp".
